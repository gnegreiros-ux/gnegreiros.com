I use artificial intelligence to produce and share my content faster. This charter states exactly what it does, what it never does, and who is accountable: me.

## Founding principle

**The final word is always human.** AI observes, proposes and accelerates. I decide, verify and sign. Nothing is published, sent or answered in my name without my approval.

## How an article is produced

1. **I choose the topic, the angle and the positions.** AI may suggest ideas; I keep or reject them. A real example: for my second article, I turned down a proposal I found too academic, in favour of a practical, concrete approach.
2. **AI writes a draft following my direction**, based on my notes and my projects.
3. **I edit and verify every fact, example and quote.** Each one points to a recognized source, cited with a direct link I have checked, or to my own direct experience.
4. **I approve and publish the final version.**

## What AI does

- Writes drafts following my direction (Claude)
- Searches and summarizes sources, which I then verify
- Edits, translates and formats
- Produces visuals and short videos, including an avatar of me reading my own scripts (HeyGen)
- Schedules the release of content I have already approved

## What AI never does

- Choose my topics, my angle or my positions
- Invent an example, a figure, a testimonial or a quote
- Create a fictional character, experience or screenshot presented as real
- Recommend a tool on my behalf
- Reply to a comment, message or email in my name. I may use a draft; I write and send the reply myself.

## What I don't publish

I never discuss my employer's work without authorization. I publish no information obtained through my employment.

## Sponsorships and affiliate links

Every sponsorship or affiliate link is clearly disclosed. I only recommend tools I actually use. I turn down any partner who would ask me to hide or downplay their own use of AI.

## Errors and corrections

AI makes mistakes, and so do I. In my first article, it invented an anecdote and misattributed a quote; I caught and fixed both before publication. If an error still gets through, I correct it publicly and date the correction. You can report an error publicly, in a comment under the content, or privately through [my website](https://gnegreiros.com/contact).

## Updates to this charter

This charter evolves with my practices and tools. Each version is dated and its changes are listed.
